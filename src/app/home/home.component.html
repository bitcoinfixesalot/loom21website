Invoices Your Accountant Can Work With

Every invoice gets the next number in one continuous sequence and prints what VAT law asks for: the tax point, both companies' IDs and VAT numbers, the tax base and, when no VAT is charged, the legal basis. Built to Bulgarian VAT rules, with PDFs in Bulgarian or English.

Switching from another program? Import your past invoices and credit notes from Excel or CSV, and Loom21's numbering carries on after your last one.

Invoicing outside Bulgaria? Most of these rules are what EU VAT law asks of any invoice. Check the Bulgarian-only ones with your accountant.
